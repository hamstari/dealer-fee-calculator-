      How the math works
      • Price = job cost ÷ (1 − overhead% − profit%). Overhead and profit are a share of the price, which is how your books measure them. 20% profit is a 25% markup on cost.
      • Financed price = your price ÷ (1 − dealer fee). On a $10,000 job with a 10% fee, charge $11,111. The lender keeps $1,111 and you get $10,000.
      • The recommended plan is the one this customer qualifies for (by credit score and loan amount) with the lowest dealer fee, or the lowest monthly payment if that's what the customer wants. The loan amount is the financed price.
      • Monthly payments are estimates. Final approval and terms come from the lender.
      • Some lenders don't allow charging financed customers more than cash customers. Check your lender agreement and local rules.
