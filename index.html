    How the math works
    • Financed price = cash price ÷ (1 − dealer fee). With a 10% fee on a $10,000 job, charge $11,111. The lender keeps $1,111 and you get $10,000.
    • With a cash down payment, the fee only applies to the financed part.
    • Overhead and profit are a % of the selling price. A 20% profit margin is a 25% markup on cost.
    • One price for all spreads the fees you expect across every job. Customers try your primary lender first; anyone it declines tries the backup.
    • Monthly payments are rough estimates. Same-as-cash and deferred-interest plans bill differently, so quote the lender's numbers.

    For estimating only. Check your lender agreement and local rules before you change how you price financed jobs.
